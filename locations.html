Visiting us

What to bring to your first visit.

Complete pre-visit forms in the patient portal beforehand to save time at the desk.

Bring your current insurance card and a government-issued photo ID. We'll verify coverage before your visit when possible.

If you wear correction, bring your current glasses and/or your contact lens boxes so we can read your existing prescription.

A list of medications you take — including eye drops — helps us provide safe, coordinated care.

Many exams include dilation, which can blur near vision for a few hours. Consider bringing sunglasses and, if you're sensitive, a driver.
